RankCloak research code and retained computational evidence for surface-form concealment of synthetic cryptographic artifacts in language-model-generated text. Version 3.0.0 contains the V4 author-review scientific snapshot and historical V1 through V3 materials. Manuscript and supplement sources, compiled PDFs, styles, bibliographies, figures, exact review correspondence, reviewer responses and cover letters are included.

The package includes generation and analysis code, frozen configurations and selections, raw and derived data, both unsuccessful semantic control-only pilots, execution and test records, reproducibility instructions, model acquisition identities and hashes, and license notices. It retains the six strict-gate failure diagnostics, exploratory local conditional-likelihood study, bounded transport diagnostics, configuration compatibility check and directional fixed-message Q4-to-Q8 byte reconstruction.

Neither semantic pilot met its prospective control-separation criterion. Conditional context gain passed its specified control-separation check and supports only limited local conditional compatibility under the retained scoring contract. Ordinary controls were sampled and RankCloak continuations were greedy. Semantic coherence and reader-perceived naturalness were not established. The results do not establish cryptographic confidentiality, statistical indistinguishability or general transport robustness. The complete written response to Reviewer 1 comment 4 has partial empirical coverage. No participant judgments were collected, and this release makes no claim of journal submission or acceptance.
