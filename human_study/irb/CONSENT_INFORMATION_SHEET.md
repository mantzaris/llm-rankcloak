# DRAFT participant information sheet — NOT IRB APPROVED

## Study title

Human assessment of English messages

## Key information

You are invited to consider a research study led by {{PI_NAME}} at the University of
Central Florida, {{UCF_DEPARTMENT}}. This draft cannot be used with participants until
UCF approves it.

The study examines how people perceive short English messages produced through
different writing and generation processes. If you join, you will read messages and
rate their grammaticality, fluency, coherence, topic adherence, completeness, overall
naturalness, and suspiciousness. The task is expected to take
{{ESTIMATED_DURATION_MINUTES}} minutes.

Participation is voluntary. You may decline or stop at any time without penalty. Your
decision will not affect any relationship with UCF. Compensation and partial-
completion handling are {{COMPENSATION_AMOUNT_AND_BASIS}}.

## What will happen

After eligibility and consent, you will receive a randomized set of 24 messages and
two instruction-attention items. Each message has a broad topic label and seven rating
questions with answers from 1 to 7. There are no correct quality ratings. We will not
ask you to write text or identify message sources.

## Risks and benefits

You may experience mild fatigue, boredom, or frustration from awkward messages. You
may skip participation by closing the survey. Online participation carries a small
privacy risk. The study avoids sensitive questions and direct identifiers where the
approved platform permits. You should not expect a direct benefit.
