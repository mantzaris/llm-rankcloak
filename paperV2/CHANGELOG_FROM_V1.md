# Changelog from the original submission

## Retained scientific core

- Retained RankCloak as the central contribution: deterministic next-token-rank transcoding of serialized cryptographic artifacts.
- Retained and clarified direct subword, ASCII-byte fixed-radix, and hex-nibble representations.
- Retained non-segmented and segmented encoding, forced-span replay, deterministic filtering, and non-payload natural tails.
- Preserved the distinction among payload capacity, forced length, full cover length, recovery, quality diagnostics, and detectability.

## Replaced pilot material

- Replaced the original partial-pilot narrative, sample sizes, figures, tables, estimates, and conclusions with the completed sealed revision evidence.
- Replaced small illustrative payload samples with 480 public deterministic outputs of eight declared cryptographic algorithms or formats, 60 per class.
- Replaced single-model or narrow-prompt reporting with three open-source 7B--8B model families and 18 English templates in six categories.
- Replaced preliminary recovery summaries with 6,480 primary payload trials, strict grouped sensitivities, 576 secondary-language trials, and complete 38,504-work-unit reconciliation.

## Added evidence and analysis

- Added controlled saved-token, visible-text-retokenization, raw-transmission, perturbation, limited-canonicalization, tail-truncation, and cross-model replay conditions.
- Added 504-stimulus automated readability and model-based quality diagnostics, explicitly separated from human evaluation.
- Added lead-in, segmentation, token-filter, and tail-policy ablations plus multiplicity-adjusted topic contrasts.
- Added formal rank, length, rate, and conditional likelihood relationships with retained-trace validation and a documented cascade-trace gap.
- Added a balanced 15,840-row neural-detector corpus, 56 completed fits, 55,440 predictions, matched and held-out regimes, leakage checks, near-duplicate sensitivity, and same-device CUDA repeatability.
- Added inclusive generation-time, throughput, effective-rate, detector-time, and peak-VRAM reporting with explicit measurement limits.
- Added Spanish and Simplified Chinese exact-copy secondary recovery tests without extending the claim to broad multilingual naturalness.

## Moved to Supplementary Information

- Moved full codec definitions, pseudocode, prompt/model identities, work-unit accounting, transformation inventory, full ablation and topic tables, detector split metrics, proofs, diagnostics, timing tables, worked examples, and the claim-boundary audit to Supplementary Notes S1--S10.
- Limited the main manuscript to four figures and two tables; copied the complete robustness, ablation, detector, and overhead figures into the self-contained V2 Supplement.

## Moderated claims and expanded limitations

- Conditioned exact recovery on saved-token exact-copy replay and stated that saved token IDs are not an ordinary text channel.
- Added the adverse findings that visible-text replay is fragile and trained neural classifiers strongly distinguish evaluated covers.
- Removed claims or implications of practical deployment, broad robustness, human-perceived naturalness, undetectability, cryptographic security, CPU/GPU equivalence, and broad multilingual generalization.
- Added explicit boundaries for zero human participants, no deployment test, unavailable work units, absent GGUF files, detector near-duplicate overlap, singular mixed models, exploratory/post-outcome analyses, incomplete perturbation coverage, timing scope, and the theory trace gap.

## References and response artifacts

- Added a point-by-point response with final page/line citations, a concise cover letter, citation and figure-provenance audits, word-count and build reports, an unresolved-items register, and an AI-assistance disclosure draft for author approval.
