The revision addresses the editor’s assessment and the reviewers’ comments through additional experiments, expanded analyses and clearer methodological descriptions. The additions include a blinded automated evaluation of contextual coherence and acceptability, detailed analysis of entropy-gating failures, further investigation of text transformations and configuration compatibility, and expanded computational and quantization analyses. The token-filtering rules are now documented in full.

These additions strengthen the evidence supporting RankCloak as a reproducible framework for representing cryptographic artifacts through language-model-generated text. The revised manuscript brings together exact recovery, contextual acceptability, efficiency and detectability within a common experimental framework. Supporting technical detail is presented in the Supplementary Information to keep the main discussion focused and readable.

The accompanying response explains how each comment has been addressed and identifies the corresponding changes. I believe the revised manuscript now provides a clear and well-supported account of the contribution and is suitable for publication in Scientific Reports.
