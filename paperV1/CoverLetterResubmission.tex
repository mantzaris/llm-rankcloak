\noindent Dear Editors of Nature Scientific Reports,
\vspace{.5cm}

\indent I am honored to be submitting this article for review in your journal which I have a high respect for.
The topic content is of importance since it builds upon recent research which shows how LLMs can be used to transcode text from one form to another via a directing prompt.
This research investigates how that approach can be used to transcode security artifacts like hashes with a modified approach. These cover texts can evade traditional detectors using the proposed methodology and the techniques for detection are also demonstrated under this new paradigm. This work is essential for the community to keep up to date with new potential cyber security tools. The theory is presented as well as the results over a collection of examples which are qualitatively conveyed as well.
