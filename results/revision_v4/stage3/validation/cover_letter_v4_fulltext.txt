The V4 revision addresses the editor’s technical assessment and Reviewer 1’s ten points while retaining Reviewer 2’s
acknowledgment and all original review wording. It adds complete token-filter documentation, six strict-gate failure
analyses with exact text windows and matched comparators, bounded transport decomposition, a configuration

conceptual block, integrity, ECC and retransmission fallback is explicitly unimplemented.
The adverse findings remain central. All 960 fixed Q4 messages fail exact recovery when decoded from saved
Q8 ranks. The transport decompositions do not establish general robustness. Neither of two control-only pilots
meets the prospective semantic control-separation requirement. Context gain passes its specified control check.
The subsequent frozen likelihood study supports limited local compatibility. Ordinary controls are sampled and
RankCloak continuations are greedy, so the contrast does not isolate a causal boundary effect. Semantic coherence
and reader-perceived naturalness remain unestablished. The response to Reviewer 1 comment 4 is complete as a
scientific explanation, with partial empirical coverage for the editor to assess. RankCloak concerns surface-form
concealment under a shared configuration and does not establish confidentiality or indistinguishability against the
evaluated trace-informed attacker.
The work is appropriate for Scientific Reports as a reproducible computational study of artifact representation,
configured inversion and measurable failure boundaries. The revision retains adverse and null results and makes
no claim of a robust or confidential communication channel.
